Why we exist

The lessons sport teaches don't stay on the field. We make sure they benefit you beyond the sport.

Sport teaches more than how to move well or race hard. Discipline. Patience. Showing up when you don't feel like it. Leading, and being led.

We don't let that stay on the field. We nurture it and carry it into how we work, how we love, how we show up — as athletes, business minds, human beings.

That's the whole point of the club. Train for the sport. Improve your life as a side effect.

Where it shows up

Business Partnership Parenting Friendship Leadership Patience Discipline Community
Where we started

Born in Zurich. Built for every sport.

We started in triathlon. But the patterns don't belong to one sport — they show up in endurance, strenght, mobility: anywhere another lesson to achieve your goals faster can be learned.

This is fueled by: a weekly Run Club in Zurich. More cities are following.
